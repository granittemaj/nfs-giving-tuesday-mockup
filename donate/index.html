Donate to Not For Sale this Giving Tuesday

The short answer

Support work against human trafficking and ecocide. This Giving Tuesday, we invite you to contribute to a mission concerned with what makes freedom possible: support after exploitation, prevention and the opportunity to build a safer livelihood.

Choose a gift that is right for you. Our official donation page provides the available giving options and the details to review before completing payment.

Understand the mission before you give

If you are new to Not For Sale, the case for support explains the approach and links to reporting. The issue guides examine human trafficking and ecocide in more detail. You can read those first or ask the team a question before deciding.

Our appeal concerns the wider mission. The actual donation service may present specific giving options, so check the purpose shown before payment. If you want a particular restriction or cannot find an option that matches your intention, contact Not For Sale for clarification. 22

Choose an amount and frequency that fit

A one-time gift is a single commitment. A recurring gift continues according to the selected schedule. Read the amount and frequency together, and consider the ongoing total before choosing. There is no need to make a commitment that creates difficulty for you.

Your gift becomes part of the support behind the work. Choose an amount you can give comfortably, and review the purpose shown with that option. If you would like to discuss a larger contribution or a particular intention, the team can help explain the available arrangements.

After your donation

Keep the confirmation and any reference number. Check that the recorded amount and frequency match your intention. If a confirmation does not arrive, check the relevant account or contact the organization before submitting a second payment, so that uncertainty does not result in an accidental duplicate gift.

If you need to change recurring support, use the instructions in the confirmation or ask through the official help route. Do not send complete payment-card details in ordinary email. A reference number and a description of the question are a safer starting point.

Employer matching and other questions

If your employer offers donation matching, check eligibility and submit the required information according to its process. Matching is separate from the original gift and should not be assumed because it is Giving Tuesday. The workplace guide includes a short inquiry you can use.

Tax treatment depends on the recipient and the rules relevant to your circumstances. Use official guidance and keep the receipt if you need a record of the gift. The donation guide explains how to check an organization and the giving terms. 05

Giving Tuesday questions
Do I have to wait until December 1?+

No. Use the official giving page when you are ready. Any separate time-limited offer should be checked according to its own terms.

Can I give monthly?+

Review the options currently available in the donation service. Confirm the recurring amount and management arrangements before payment.

Does this page take my card details?+

No. The action sends you to Not For Sale’s official donation page to review and complete the gift.

Can I choose a specific purpose?+

Check the options and terms in the giving service. Contact the team before paying if you need a restriction that is not clearly offered.

Will my gift be matched?+

Matching depends on a separate employer program or announced campaign. Check its terms to see whether your gift qualifies.

What if I need help with a payment?+

Use the contact information in your confirmation or the official contact page. Include the reference number where available, not full card details.

Can I send a Gift of Giving now?+

The Gift of Giving page is a concept demonstration. It does not process gifts, send recipient links or record choices.

Can I help without donating?+

Yes. Explore Giving Tuesday ideas or volunteering for other ways to contribute.
